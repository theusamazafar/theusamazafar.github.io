About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Information Management at National Chung Cheng University , Taiwan, where I conduct my doctoral research under the supervision of Prof. Fan Wu (吳帆) .

My academic background spans Information Management, Control Science & Engineering, and Software Engineering. My work combines technical AI methods with information systems perspectives to study trustworthy and practically meaningful intelligent systems.

Research Interests

My research interests include explainable artificial intelligence, machine learning, anomaly detection, information security, FinTech, and information systems. I am particularly interested in the transparency, reliability, and evaluation of AI-supported systems in real-world decision-making environments.
