About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Information Management at National Chung Cheng University , Taiwan. I conduct my doctoral research under the supervision of Prof. Fan Wu (吳帆) .

My work focuses on how artificial intelligence can support better decisions in information systems, particularly when users need to understand why a model produced a particular result.

Research Interests

I am interested in applying explainable and trustworthy AI to information systems where model outputs influence real decisions. My current work examines applications such as fraud detection, healthcare decision support, anomaly detection, and AI-generated or manipulated digital content.

Across these settings, I am particularly interested in how explanations can make AI systems more transparent and useful, how model reliability and fairness change over time, and how people respond to AI-generated information when trust and uncertainty are involved.
