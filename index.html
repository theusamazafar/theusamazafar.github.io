About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Information Management at National Chung Cheng University , Taiwan, under the supervision of Prof. Fan Wu (吳帆) . My research focuses on the use of artificial intelligence in information systems, particularly for decision support, explainability, trust, and system reliability.

Research Interests & Expertise

My research interests include explainable AI, trustworthy AI, machine learning, healthcare informatics, information security, anomaly detection, and FinTech.

Current projects include healthcare decision support, fraud detection, AI-generated content, e-commerce anomaly detection, and autonomous clinical agents. I am also interested in how explanations influence trust and decision-making, and how fairness, reliability, and data changes affect AI system performance over time.
