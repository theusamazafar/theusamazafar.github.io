About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Information Management at National Chung Cheng University (CCU), Taiwan.

I am pursuing my doctoral research under the supervision of Prof. Fan Wu , Professor in the Department of Information Management at National Chung Cheng University.

My research interests include explainable artificial intelligence (XAI), anomaly detection, information security, fintech, artificial intelligence, and applied machine learning.

My interdisciplinary academic background spans Information Management, Control Science & Engineering, and Software Engineering. I also have experience in web development, academic teaching, and research.

My broader research goal is to contribute to the development and evaluation of transparent, trustworthy, and practically meaningful AI-enabled information systems.
