🎓 About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Information Management at National Chung Cheng University , Taiwan, where I conduct my doctoral research under the supervision of Prof. Fan Wu (吳帆) .

My academic background spans Information Management, Control Science & Engineering, and Software Engineering. My research focuses on the development and evaluation of trustworthy AI-enabled information systems.

🔬 Research Interests

My research interests include explainable artificial intelligence, machine learning, anomaly detection, information security, FinTech, and information systems. I am particularly interested in transparent and trustworthy AI approaches for real-world decision-making environments.
