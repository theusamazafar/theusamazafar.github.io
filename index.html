About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Information Management at National Chung Cheng University in Taiwan.

My academic work spans explainable artificial intelligence, anomaly detection, information security, fintech, and applied machine learning. I am interested in research that combines methodological rigor with practical relevance and helps make AI-supported systems more transparent, reliable, and useful.

My interdisciplinary background includes Information Management, Control Science and Engineering, and Software Engineering, together with experience in web development, teaching, and academic research.
