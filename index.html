About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Information Management at National Chung Cheng University , Taiwan, under the supervision of Prof. Fan Wu (吳帆) . My academic work combines information systems research with applied artificial intelligence, with a focus on developing and evaluating AI-based approaches for practical decision problems.

Research Interests

My research interests include explainable AI, trustworthy AI, machine learning, information security, anomaly detection, and FinTech. Current projects include healthcare decision support, fraud detection, AI-generated content, and autonomous clinical agents.

I am also interested in how explanations affect trust and decision-making, and how fairness, reliability, and changes in data influence AI system performance over time.
