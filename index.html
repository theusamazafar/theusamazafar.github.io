About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Information Management at National Chung Cheng University (CCU), Taiwan, where I conduct my doctoral research under the supervision of Prof. Fan Wu (吳帆) .

My interdisciplinary academic background spans Information Management, Control Science & Engineering, and Software Engineering. This background allows me to approach research questions from both technical and information-systems perspectives.

Alongside research, I have experience in university teaching and academic support as well as several years of professional web development experience.
